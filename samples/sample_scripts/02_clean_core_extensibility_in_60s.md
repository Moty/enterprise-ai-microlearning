## LinkedIn Post Copy

Still confused about what SAP "Clean Core" actually means in practice?

Here is the 60-second architectural rule of thumb every enterprise leader and developer needs to memorize:

🟢 **Tier 1 (Key-User):** Low-code/no-code UI tweaks and custom fields directly in Fiori apps. Zero upgrade risk.  
🔵 **Tier 2 (On-Stack Developer):** Custom ABAP Cloud extensions running directly on S/4HANA, but restricted strictly to SAP-released APIs and Clean Core rules.  
🟣 **Tier 3 (Side-by-Side on BTP):** Complex third-party integrations, custom microservices, and decoupled logic running on SAP Business Technology Platform.

Stop modifying standard SAP objects. Keep the core clean, and your quarterly upgrades become seamless.

💬 How strict is your current Clean Core governance? Are your developers embracing ABAP Cloud or sticking to classic enhancements?
